# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Jack and people opening a shared link who want a concise record of a small project,
technical decision, or experiment.

## Product Purpose

Publish durable, easy-to-scan static write-ups and small projects from one simple
GitHub Pages index.

## Brand Personality

Direct, practical, compact.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a dashboard, marketing template, glossy portfolio, or decorative
editorial spread. Avoid ornamental metadata, rounded cards, gradients, shadows,
badges, and filler.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the result.
- Make each page understandable without prior conversation.
- Show the artifact when the artifact is visual.
- Keep implementation small and durable.
- Prefer useful detail over decorative structure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, high-contrast text, visible links and controls, useful alt text,
and layouts that remain readable at 390 pixels wide.
